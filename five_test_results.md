## Overall findings

The pipeline is reliable enough to complete all five scans and produces polished reports with strong profile extraction, agent activity, agency translation, historical evidence, and source links. Advanced manufacturing was the strongest case. The consumer/workforce result is the clearest blocker for winning because the challenge explicitly rewards honest negative results. Climate/water also exposed a ranking problem: topical words can elevate semantically adjacent but substantively irrelevant programs. The current report should be treated as a strong prototype with a real matching-quality gap, not yet as evidence of accurate eligibility recommendations.
